## 1) Business Question
Which acquisition channels drive sustainable revenue growth without harming retention?

## 2) Data
- Ecommerce customer, session, and order data (2024)
- Retention defined as repeat purchase in subsequent months

## 3) Key Findings
- Referral channel shows the highest retention and above-average AOV.
- Paid Search converts well initially but retains worse after month 2.
- Customers with refunds are significantly more likely to churn.

## 4) Implications
Optimizing purely for conversion increases short-term revenue but reduces
customer lifetime value. Channel quality matters more than volume.

## 5) Recommendations
- Shift budget from Social to Referral and Email channels.
- Improve post-purchase experience to reduce refunds.
- Track retention by acquisition channel monthly.

## 6) Risks & Next Steps
- Validate findings with real production data.
- Extend analysis to lifetime value modeling.
